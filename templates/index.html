How It Works

Protection in three steps

From keystroke to safety decision in under 15 milliseconds. No friction, no latency, no compromise.

1

Type

A user types a message or uploads an image. CyberShield's lightweight hook captures the input in real time — zero storage, zero delay.

2

Analyze

BERT processes text semantics while ResNet scans image content. The fusion model scores across 6 toxicity dimensions in parallel — under 15ms.

3

Protect

Based on your policy thresholds, CyberShield blocks, warns, or flags content — notifying moderators and protecting users automatically.
